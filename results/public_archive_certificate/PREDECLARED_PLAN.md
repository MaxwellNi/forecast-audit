# Finite public archive certificate: plan before audit outputs

Use the first 96 electricity meters in the existing UCI public source. Train a fixed histogram gradient boosting model only on daily targets before 2013. Freeze its 2013–2014 forecasts, the previous-week forecasts, and a four-category baseline/calendar control before randomized audit sampling. The categories split the previous-week forecast at its chronological training median and distinguish weekdays from weekends. The estimand adjusts for these four categories; it does not adjust for the entire continuous previous-week forecast.

The three fixed candidate scores are HGB, the raw previous-week forecast, and the category code itself. Including the raw previous-week forecast exposes residual information left by coarse categories. The category code has zero conditional residual target by construction and stays in the complete three-member family.

Condition on the resulting fixed 70,080-row archive. Sample row indices independently and uniformly with replacement: 4,096 nuisance-training rows, 8,192 validation pairs (16,384 row queries), and 12,288 evaluation rows, initially arranged as 128 groups of 96. The 32,768 total queries, including both validation-pair members, are shared by all methods and candidates. Repeated indices are retained. Independence is generated by the sampling design conditional on the archive; the original time series is not assumed independent.

The primary certificate uses the pooled full order-three U statistic, signed learning allowance and the proved variance bound. Retain the grouped full-U absolute/range baseline, signed/range and signed/variance versions, pooled/range comparator, and independent-triple range and Bernstein comparators on exactly the same sampled rows. Each method reports complete-family BY over the three declared candidates at 0.05, with learning failure allowance 0.0001. No method will be selected after its results are seen.

Write the certificate outputs before computing full-census midranks, category means and exact archive theta. Report the census as an exact alternative, not a training oracle. Count total and unique sampled row queries, census rows and computation time. Archive preparation already accesses and materializes the full archive, so this implementation cannot claim end-to-end data-access savings even when its statistical sampling budget is below census size.

This is a locally frozen new analysis of an already used historical dataset. It is not external preregistration, a fresh independent real-world confirmation, future-time risk certification, or a population statement beyond the fixed archive.
